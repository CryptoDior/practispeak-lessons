⚡ Quick Grammar Reminder
should = strong advice could = softer suggestion might = uncertain warning can / can't = ability or ruling out
Modal verbs (should / could / might / can) go before the main verb and show how strong or certain your idea is.
If-sentences: If + present tense, will/won't + action
→ "If we push now, we'll catch them."
⚠ The if-part is always present tense — never "if we will push."
